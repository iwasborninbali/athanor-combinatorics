**Scope and interpretation.** The target was a published or preprinted
upper bound at most 104 for C_2(6,3,2), and a published lower bound above 99.
Parameter translations searched were the conventional T_2(6,4,3), Pavese's
reversed Turán notation T_2(6,3,4), and (3,2)-blocking sets of planes in
PG(5,2). I also checked the complement problem
A_2(6,3,2;14) = B_2(6,3,2;15) >= 1291. Ordinary set-covering numbers,
distance-constrained packing codes, point blocking sets, and metric covering
radii were distinguished by their definitions.
