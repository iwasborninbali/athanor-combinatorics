# Independent audit incorporated into the paper

This is a packaging summary of the independent a05 verification dated
11 November 2026 UTC. The original report and detailed priority-search record
are preserved verbatim as plain text in `audit/verify-original.txt` and
`audit/search_record.txt`. Those original reports' directory-relative paths
refer to the research archive; the executable bundle instructions are in
[README.md](README.md).

## Mathematical verdict

The verifier's witness verdict was **confirmed**. Its separately written
standard-library script imported no search code or stored incidence data.
It formed each block by XOR closure, checked rank three and uniqueness,
generated all 651 two-spaces as point triples, and directly checked coverage.
It also verified the dual condition: every four-space contains an
orthogonal-complement block. Two internal checkers and the Guide's own script
also confirmed the witness; the Guide-script confirmation is part of the
supplied research handoff, rather than a script replay in this package.

The 104-cover's line multiplicity histogram is
`{1: 616, 2: 3, 3: 28, 4: 2, 6: 1, 8: 1}`. Its private-line histogram per
block is `{4: 28, 5: 8, 6: 12, 7: 56}`. The certificate is therefore
inclusion-minimal, without a conclusion of optimal cardinality.

The audit independently determined the **full** GL(6,2) stabilizer to be
C₂ × C₂, generated by maps with standard-basis images
`[15,29,10,25,30,49]` and `[39,36,51,25,54,49]`. Its exhaustive test used
invariant point colors refined from point degree and joining-line cover
multiplicity, then enumerated all compatible images of a fixed domain basis.
Exactly 4,096 full image bases survived the necessary tests; exactly four
preserved the family. No stabilizing correlation exists, since 36 four-spaces
contain no original block. The bundled `checker.py` recomputes these facts
from the original witness and retains no dependency on saved color classes.

## Priority verdict and limitations

The priority verdict was **provisionally new**. No published or arXiv-posted
upper bound at most 104, or lower bound above 99, was located. The smallest
checked published upper bound was 105. The initial investigation began its
bounded search approximately 00:53–00:56 UTC; the independent priority audit
began approximately 01:11 UTC on 11 November 2026.

The prior-art chain is explicit:

1. Etzion, *Covering of subspaces by subspaces* (2014), p. 417: the table cell
   gives 99–106. The auditor inspected the table visually.
2. Cossidente–Pavese, *A Note on q-Covering Designs in PG(5,q)* (2016),
   Theorem 2.5: the 105 predecessor. The publisher metadata and abstract were
   accessible, while the institutional full-text request returned 403.
3. Pavese, *On q-Covering Designs* (2020), Construction 4 and Theorem 5, p. 7:
   explicitly restates and attributes the 2016 construction of size
   q⁶ + q⁴ + 2q³ + 2q² + q − 1, equal to 105 at q=2. This was the actual
   primary mathematical text used to check and reconstruct the predecessor.

Searches covered exact parameter variants, q-Turán designs and blocking
planes, as well as complementary packings: a 104-cover is complementary to
1,291 three-spaces with two-space multiplicity at most 14. Conventional dual
notation is T₂(6,4,3); Pavese reverses the final two Turán parameters. The
dual question is distinct from solids covering all planes.

The auditor checked historical tables, author publication lists, arXiv
queries and citation chains, including live OpenAlex lists with 13 citers of
Etzion (2014), two of Cossidente–Pavese (2016), and one of Pavese (2020).
Relevant full-text checks were distinguished from title/abstract screening.
The 2025 Kovács–Nagy–Szabó improvements concern another parameter family;
no applicable ≤104 specialization was located. The audit did not optimize
every free choice of every general construction.

Access to the live Bayreuth website timed out. Its technical report concerns
distance-constrained subspace-code tables; the current live contents were
not checked. Citation indexing is incomplete, and unindexed, inaccessible,
unpublished or differently phrased results cannot be ruled out. Earlier
retrieval mistakes were recorded and excluded, including wrong arXiv
identifiers and incorrectly encoded API requests. The corrected requests
support the search record. **No priority is claimed beyond this dated audit.**

## What the package establishes

The clean replay verifies the positive witness and its complete ambient
stabilizer. It supplies no exclusion of sizes 99–103 and no general
classification of covers. Historical search reports and source hashes
document provenance and bounded priority checking; they are not mathematical
certificates of novelty.
